
============================================================
PROG2004 Assessment 1 - Health Service Appointment System
============================================================
A scripted demonstration of the object-oriented backbone of a
health service appointment system. There is no menu or user
input: main creates the objects and exercises their behaviour.

Parts 1 and 2 are the class design itself: HealthProfessional is
an abstract class holding the shared data, and the roster contains
GeneralPractitioner, Specialist, Nurse and Dietitian objects.
Part 3 exercises that design below.
